I wasn’t trying to build more software. I was trying to stop paying for more than I needed.

That became the brief: bring the jobs I actually use into one connected site. Give couples one place to start, and give me a clearer view of the work behind it.

01 / THE CLIENT SIDE

A path for each couple.

A personal page connecting their wedding website and delivered films. Photo galleries and film-only delivery have their own presentation.

02 / MY SIDE

The job stays in view.

Leads, projects, payment records, to-dos, and planning information come together in a dashboard I helped shape around my work.

03 / MY ROLE

I directed. AI helped build.

I set the requirements and reviewed the result. Claude Code wrote the implementation, with Codex contributing a security review. I still own the decisions and the upkeep.

The cost question: A fair savings comparison needs my actual old bills and current hosting, storage, and email usage. Owning the code still comes with maintenance and running costs.
