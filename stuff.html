Next to Kin

PORTFOLIO / NOT LIVE

Concepts and prototypes.

These aren’t live sites and none of them is a finished client project. They show range: what a museum, a phone shop or a product idea could look like.

CONCEPT · UNOFFICIAL · NOT LIVE

Bresaylor Heritage Museum

An unofficial redesign idea for a volunteer-run Métis historic site. Visit planning sits beside a three-episode radio serial voiced and scored with AI tools, a numbered collection ledger, and a cart-trail game. The radio page labels what is documented, remembered and invented.

DEMO · NOT LIVE

Cell Style

A demo site for a phone repair and resale shop. A short guide asks about your situation and points you to repair, a used phone or a simpler phone. It comes with seven matching social posts.

PROTOTYPE · NOT LIVE

Let’s Pucking Go

A designer for hockey bag tags shaped like mini pucks. Set the name, number and team colour on a 3D preview, then download a file ready for a colour 3D printer.

CONCEPT · NOT LIVE

Homegrain

A storefront idea for wooden engravings of a customer’s house, made from a photo. A before and after, a sample gallery clearly marked as AI concepts, and a request form.

CONCEPT · SAMPLE DATA · NOT LIVE

Distilled Democracy

An interactive mock-up of a news-accountability idea. Seat experts from across the spectrum at a round table and watch scripted answers play out. Every outlet, grade and expert is a fictional sample.

PROTOTYPE · NOT LIVE

Marquee Studio

A generator for laser-cut wooden marquee signs. An early prototype of a design-it-yourself tool for a physical product.
